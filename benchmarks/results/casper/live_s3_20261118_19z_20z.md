# First realtime runs published to S3: HRRR 2026-11-18 19Z and 20Z

The first runs uploaded to `s3://wwe-ptype` with the scoped realtime key (`docs/aws_realtime.md`), at commit
`0aee713`. The earlier live run (`live_20261118_16z.md`) wrote to a directory standing in for the bucket, so
this is the first test of the IAM policy and of the boto3 per-file upload against S3.

Both jobs: Casper `htc`, 6 cores, 24 GB, 1 worker of 4 threads, account NAML0001, started by
`applications/hrrr_ptype_realtime_submit.py` run by hand (`--lead-minutes 20 --no-catchup`, lookback 1 h for
20Z and 2 h for 19Z).

## The key

Checked from a Derecho login node before the jobs:

| Request | Result |
|---|---|
| `head-object ptype/inits.json` | succeeded |
| list the bucket root | refused (`AccessDenied` on `s3:ListBucket`) |
| list `ptype/`, `ptype/realtime-staging/`, `soundings/` | succeeded |

Put, Get and the conditional index rewrite were exercised by the jobs. **Delete was not**: staging stores
are removed by a later job once they are six hours old, and both were still in the bucket afterwards
(2,626 objects, 120 MB, Standard class).

  That is 6.0 core-hours for an 18-hour cycle.

## 19Z: started after its files were all there (job 6741605)

Submitted 96 minutes after initialization, so every hour was ready and the job ran flat out.

- 19 hours published between 20:37:18 (worker ready) and 20:42:43, then consolidated and indexed at 20:43:41:
  6.7 minutes from the run starting.
- Consolidation took 47.0 s here against 8.0 s for 20Z; the cause was not looked into.
- Walltime 9 min 48 s, CPU time 16 min, peak memory 8.1 GB.
- The status file said `running` until 20:46:20, 2 min 40 s after the index was final: the last status write
  comes after the local stores are removed.
